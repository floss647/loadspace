# Held reviews

Reviews that passed fact-checking but are too thin to publish yet (no confirmed specs, no road test of the current van). They live here, outside src/pages, so they aren't built. To publish one, confirm its specs from the maker's current UK price list, re-run the fact-check, and move it back to src/pages/reviews/.
