notes:
  - "Each payload is the 3,500kg gross weight minus the van's CVW. Maxus doesn't explain what its CVW includes or say whether the payload allows for a driver, so ask the dealer before you load to the limit."
  - "Maxus gives the gross train weight as 5,000kg but says to refer to the VIN plate, so check the plate on the van itself."
  - "The 43-minute rapid charge time is for 20 to 80% on a 90kW, 150A DC charger. Maxus doesn't give a peak charging power."
  - "The 150kW figure is the motor's power, not a charging speed."
  - "AC charging times are from 5 to 100%. Maxus gives times on 11kW and 22kW chargers, but doesn't say how fast the van's onboard charger is."
  - "Maxus lists a maximum length, width and height without saying what they measure. They are much smaller than the van's outside size, so we show them as load space figures."
  - "Maxus's guide has no prices and no warranty details. It marks the options above as optional but doesn't price them."
  - "Maxus lists two smart keys for every version except the L1 H1 with the 88kWh battery, whose pages don't mention them."
