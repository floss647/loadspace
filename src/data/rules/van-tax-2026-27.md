These are the UK tax and grant rules our [calculators](/tools/) use for 2026/27. Check your own position with your accountant.

### Road tax

A diesel, petrol or electric van up to 3,500kg is £360 a year from 1 April 2026 to 31 March 2027. Electric vans moved onto the standard van rate on 1 April 2025, so they pay the same as diesel.

### The van grant

The Zero Emission Van Grant takes up to 35% off the price of an eligible new electric van, to a maximum of £2,500 for a small van (under 2,500kg gross vehicle weight) or £5,000 for a large van (2,500kg to 4,250kg). You don't apply for it: the dealer takes it off the price. Only listed models qualify.

### Company vans: the driver's tax

If an employee can use a company diesel or petrol van privately, they pay income tax on a van benefit charge of £4,170, at their own rate: 20% basic, 40% higher or 45% additional rate (Scotland's bands differ). If the business also pays for private fuel, a van fuel benefit charge of £798 is added. Both are nil (£0) for a zero-emission van.

There's no charge if the van is used privately only for commuting and the terms of use forbid other private use, or if private use is insignificant, such as a small detour for a newspaper on the way to work.

### Buying or leasing a van

The Annual Investment Allowance lets a business deduct equipment spending from profits, up to £1 million a year. Vans qualify, diesel or electric, new or used; cars don't. Limited companies can instead claim full expensing, which deducts 100% of the cost, but only on new and unused vans. Any business can claim a 40% first-year allowance on new and unused vans bought on or after 1 January 2026. Sole traders and partnerships using cash basis claim a van's cost as an allowable expense instead. If you sell a van after claiming the allowance, you may need to pay tax.

The 100% first-year allowance for zero-emission goods vehicles only covered vans bought before April 2025, so electric vans now get the same relief as diesel.

Lease rentals on a van count as an allowable business expense.

### Tax rates on profits

Companies pay corporation tax at 19% on profits up to £50,000 and 25% on profits over £250,000. In between, marginal relief applies, making the rate on each extra pound 26.5%.

Sole traders and partners pay income tax plus Class 4 National Insurance of 6% on profits between £12,570 and £50,270 and 2% above that. So the rate on each extra pound is 26% for basic-rate, 42% for higher-rate and 47% for additional-rate taxpayers.

### Double-cab pickups

From April 2025, most double-cab pickups count as cars for capital allowances and company vehicle tax: no Annual Investment Allowance, and the car benefit charge instead of the van charge. Transitional rules: for capital allowances, contracts made before 1 April 2025 (6 April for income tax) with spending before 1 October 2025; for company vehicle tax, pickups bought, leased or ordered before 6 April 2025 keep the van treatment until they're sold, the lease ends or 5 April 2029, whichever comes first.

### Budget 2026: changes not yet in force

- From 1 January 2027, the first-year allowance on new vans rises from 40% to 50%, once the Finance Bill becomes law.
- From 1 April 2027, van road tax rises to £375 a year.
- In the 2027 to 2028 tax year, the van benefit charge rises to £4,310 and the van fuel benefit charge to £825.
