This week I've been thinking about lions. Specifically, about whether a large van should look as if it's had a fight with one, and won the argument.

## The van with claw marks

Van Reviewer reports that MAN has added a new version of its TGE large van called the Individual Lion X. It gets matt black trim, optional black-painted bull bars and side steps, and, my favourite, claw-mark graphics on the mirror caps.

Claw marks. On the mirrors. As though somewhere between the depot and the first drop it was set upon by something large and furry, and came off best. Inside, there's an X-branded entry handle and a removable mat for the door step. So the lion wipes its feet before it gets in. That's the most British thing I've heard about a German van.

There's a serious bit too. MAN's new Performance Kit lifts the heavy-duty engine from 163hp to 190hp, so 27 more horsepower, which in my experience means arriving at the same traffic jam slightly sooner.

## Two umpires, one match

On Wednesday, Greenfleet reported that the Climate Change Committee warned the UK is likely to miss its legal climate targets if the government weakens the Zero-Emission Vehicle Mandate. The mandate, which covers vans as well as cars, requires makers to sell a rising share of electric vehicles each year, and the committee set out its view in a letter to the Department for Transport. Its chair, Nigel Topping, said the UK currently ranks 22nd in the world for EV sales.

The same day, SMMT's Mike Hawes replied that "it is worth noting that no major global market ahead of the UK has a mandate", and that "The industry cannot subsidise the transition indefinitely".

Put the two side by side and the league table gets interesting. We're 22nd, and SMMT says no major market above us has a mandate. So our mandate is either the reason we're not thirtieth or the reason we're not tenth, depending on who's holding the microphone. It's like two umpires watching the same ball and one shouting "out" while the other shouts "sandwich". I'd hate to be the government, which has to make the call while both of them stare at it.

## The 6%

A survey commissioned by the leasing company Alphabet, reported by Van Reviewer, asked company car and van drivers about stress. 19% feel stressed every time they get behind the wheel. Heavy traffic was the biggest single cause, named by 33%, followed by poor road conditions on 22%. Among delivery workers, 48% feel stressed at least once a week.

And then there's the 6% who said they never find driving stressful. I want to meet these people. I suspect they're in one of sixty-four parking spaces outside a garden centre, engine off, flask open, watching everyone else. Either that or they've just bought a Lion X, and the other drivers are the stressed ones.

## The 100,000th was a car

Finally, Van Reviewer reports Vauxhall has passed 100,000 electric vehicle sales in the UK, and had sold 102,852 by October. Vauxhall was the UK's best-selling electric van maker three years running, from 2021 to 2023, and the Combo Electric van is built at Ellesmere Port.

And the milestone vehicle? A Grandland Electric 213PS Ultimate. A car. On a van website I'm duty-bound to point this out. Somewhere in Cheshire, a Combo Electric is muttering into its load space about who did all the early work.

That's the column. The Polo mint is still in the glovebox, still unstressed, firmly among the 6%. I've ordered it a claw-mark sticker. See you next Friday.
