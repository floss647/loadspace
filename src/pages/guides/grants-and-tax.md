## Plug-in Van and Truck Grants

Discounts applied by the dealer on eligible **new** electric vans and trucks. In 2025/26 the maximums were:

| Vehicle | Maximum grant (2025/26) |
|---|---|
| Small vans (under 2.5 tonnes) | £2,500 |
| Large vans (2.5–4.25 tonnes) | £5,000 |
| Small trucks (4.25–12 tonnes) | £16,000 |
| Large trucks (over 12 tonnes) | £25,000 |

The scheme has been **extended into 2026/27**, but the government said the new amounts would be "confirmed in due course". Ask the dealer what applies on the day you order. Used vehicles don't qualify.

## VAT

VAT-registered businesses can usually reclaim the VAT on a van, whether bought or leased, if it's used for the business. If you're **not VAT-registered**, you pay 20% VAT on lease payments with no way to reclaim it. That can make buying used, from a private seller with no VAT, much better value. ## Company van tax (van benefit)

When drivers can use a company van privately, a taxable "van benefit" usually applies. **Zero-emission vans have no van benefit charge**, a big saving for staff who take vans home. Check current figures on GOV.UK.

## Road tax (VED)

Electric vans have paid the standard van rate since April 2025.

## Capital allowances

Buying a **new** zero-emission van can qualify for a 100% first-year allowance, so you can deduct the full cost from profits in the year you buy. Used vans usually go through the Annual Investment Allowance instead. Ask your accountant which applies to you.

## London charges

- **Congestion Charge:** since January 2026, electric vans registered for Auto Pay get 50% off (£9 a day, not £18). This falls to 25% from March 2030.
- **ULEZ and clean air zones** in other cities don't charge electric vans, but modern Euro 6 diesels are also usually exempt.

## Driving licence

A car licence covers electric vans up to **4.25 tonnes** (3.5 tonnes for diesel). [Van sizes and licences explained](/guides/van-sizes-explained/)
