## Where things stand

Zero-emission trucks made up only about **1% of new truck registrations** in the first nine months of 2026. Sales fell after government funding for large trials ended. The industry says high prices and slow grid connections for depots are the main barriers.

But big operators are moving: Amazon has ordered more than 140 Mercedes eActros 600 tractor units for the UK, the largest single electric truck order in Britain so far.

## Where electric trucks work today

- **Urban and regional distribution** with fixed routes that return to base.
- **Refuse and municipal work**, which is stop-start, low speed and back to the depot every day.
- **Back-to-base trunking** with charging at both ends.
- **Operators with customers asking for zero-emission delivery.**

## Where it's harder

- **Long, unpredictable routes** without guaranteed charging.
- **Heavy payloads** where battery weight eats into what you can carry.
- **Sites where you can't get enough power** in a reasonable time.

## Questions to ask before you order

1. What's our **longest regular day**, and does the truck's real-world range cover it with a margin?
2. How much **payload** do we lose to the battery?
3. Can our **depot** supply the power, and when?
4. What **grant** applies, and what's the total cost over the life of the truck, compared with diesel?
5. What **service and repair support** does the dealer offer for electric trucks?
