## The short answer

- **Want fixed costs and a new van every few years?** Contract hire (leasing).
- **Want to own it eventually and keep it for years?** Hire purchase (HP) or cash.
- **Doing very high or unpredictable miles?** Buying usually beats leasing, because lease mileage limits are expensive to break.

## The four options

| | Contract hire | Finance lease | Hire purchase | Cash |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Own it at the end? | No, hand it back | No, but you sell it and share the proceeds | Yes, after the final payment | Yes, from day one |
| Monthly cost | Lowest | Low | Higher | None |
| Mileage limit | Yes | Usually no | No | No |
| Risk if values fall | The leasing company's | Yours | Yours | Yours |
| Damage charges at the end | Yes | No | No | No |
| Who it's for | Businesses that want fixed costs | Businesses comfortable with resale risk | Anyone wanting to own | Anyone with the cash |

## Contract hire, explained

You pay an **upfront payment** (often 3, 6, 9 or 12 times the monthly price), then a fixed monthly payment, and hand the van back at the end.

The headline price can be misleading. A lease written as **"6 + 35"** means six monthly payments upfront, then 35 more. A lower monthly price with a bigger upfront payment is not always cheaper. Use our [lease true-cost calculator](/tools/lease-true-cost/) to compare deals properly.

**Watch for:**
- **Mileage limits.** Going over costs a set amount per mile. Be honest about your miles at the start.
- **End-of-lease damage charges.** Most leasing companies use the BVRLA "fair wear and tear" standard. Ply-line the load area and keep photos at collection and return.
- **Maintenance packages.** Often worth it for high-mileage vans; less so for low-mileage ones.

## Hire purchase, explained

You pay a deposit, then monthly payments, and own the van after the last one. No mileage limits and no damage charges, but you carry the risk of what it's worth when you sell.

## VAT and tax: the basics

Vans are treated more kindly than cars:
- **VAT-registered businesses can usually reclaim the VAT** on a van, whether leased or bought, as long as it's used for the business.
- **Buying (including on HP)** usually lets you claim capital allowances against profits.
- **Leasing** payments are usually a business expense.

Rules depend on your business and change over time. **Check with your accountant before you sign.** Pickups are treated differently: double-cab pickups have been taxed like cars for many purposes since April 2025.
