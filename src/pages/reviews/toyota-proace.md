### Who it's for

The Proace Electric is Toyota's badge on the Stellantis medium van, the same family as the [Citroën ë-Dispatch](/vans/citroen-dispatch/), [Peugeot E-Expert](/vans/peugeot-expert/), [Vauxhall Vivaro Electric](/vans/vauxhall-vivaro/) and [Fiat E-Scudo](/vans/fiat-scudo/). Buyers' guides list it as part of the same family, so for the shared body, cab and drivetrain, read our [Vivaro Electric](/reviews/vauxhall-vivaro/) and [ë-Dispatch](/reviews/citroen-dispatch/) reviews. The Toyota is for the operator who keeps vans a long time and wants a manufacturer warranty for as long as possible.

We should be plain about what we could and couldn't confirm. We found no published UK road test of the current electric Proace, and Toyota's own UK site and press office couldn't be reached to check its price list or spec sheet. This review sticks to what is published.

### The warranty

This is the main reason to choose the Toyota. The standard cover is three years or 60,000 miles, but every time the van is serviced by Toyota the warranty is extended to the next service, until the van is 10 years old or has covered 100,000 miles. Toyota says the scheme covers electric models as well as diesels, and it can even be picked up on a used Toyota van that is out of warranty. The catch is that you have to keep using Toyota dealers, and Toyota servicing isn't the cheapest. New Toyota vans also get five years of roadside assistance.

We couldn't confirm the current battery warranty. Before the update, the Proace Electric's battery was covered for eight years or 100,000 miles, in line with most rivals, but check the current terms in writing.

### Range, charging and kit

In February 2024 Toyota launched the facelifted diesel Proace, with Active, Icon, Icon Premium and Sport trims and a 10-inch touchscreen even on the entry Active trim. We couldn't confirm which of those trims, or what kit, the electric van gets now.

The same goes for range and payload. The electric van before the update was rated at 205 miles from its 75kWh battery, with 100kW rapid charging. Its updated twins quote between 217 and 231 miles, but we can't assume the Toyota figure matches, so ask the dealer for the official WLTP range and plated payload of the exact van. Our [charging guide](/guides/charging-an-electric-van/) explains what to look for.

### Price and alternatives

We couldn't find a current UK price. Reports agree the Toyota tends to be pricier because of its warranty, so get quotes on all five badges and set the extra cost against the longer cover. It is on the government's list of large vans eligible for the plug-in van grant, which is worth up to £5,000; see our [grants guide](/guides/grants-and-tax/). Outside the family, compare the [Ford E-Transit Custom](/vans/ford-transit-custom/) and [Kia PV5 Cargo](/vans/kia-pv5-cargo/), and use our [electric vs diesel calculator](/tools/electric-vs-diesel/) to check the running costs.
