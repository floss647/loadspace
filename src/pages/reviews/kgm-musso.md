---

The Musso EV borrows a famous pickup name but very little else. It is built on KGM's car platform rather than a separate chassis and shares almost nothing with the diesel Musso. That explains both its strengths and its limits.

### Who it's for

Private buyers, company car drivers and light-duty businesses who want an electric four-wheel-drive pickup without paying around £60,000 before VAT, the reported price of an Isuzu D-Max EV. If you carry heavy materials or tow big plant, look at the [Isuzu D-Max EV](/vans/isuzu-d-max/) instead.

### Load bed, payload and towing

It is double cab only. The bed is 1,345mm long, 1,515mm wide and 510mm deep, narrowing to 1,110mm between the arches, and it still takes a Euro pallet. A load liner comes fitted, and there's self-levelling rear suspension.

KGM gives the payload as 690 kg and braked towing as 2,300kg. Either way it falls well short of a tonne and of the usual 3.5-tonne towing.

### Tax: van or car?

HMRC's VAT approach sorts double cab pickups by payload: under one tonne counts as a car, a tonne or more as a van. Payload for this test is gross weight minus kerb weight, and fitting accessories can lower it. HMRC's VAT manual also says most ordinary business cars carry a block on reclaiming the input tax. Every reported Musso EV payload is well under a tonne, so don't plan on reclaiming the VAT without advice.

For benefit in kind, HMRC says that from 6 April 2025 most double cab pickups are expected to be classified as cars, judged on the vehicle as a whole, with transitional rules for some pickups bought, leased or ordered before that date. With zero tailpipe emissions it is taxed like an electric car, which helps: one estimate puts it from about £30 a month in company car tax in 2026/27, against £227 for a diesel Musso. Your own position depends on how you buy and use it, so check with your accountant. See our [grants and tax guide](/guides/grants-and-tax/).

### Range and charging in the real world

The 80.6kWh battery is bigger than its rivals'. KGM quotes up to 235 miles of WLTP combined range and up to 290 miles in city driving, though another KGM page gives 240 miles without naming the test. One road test returned 2.5 miles per kWh on the motorway and up to 2.9 on local runs, which suggests the official figure is within reach.

KGM gives 120kW rapid charging, taking the battery from 20 to 80 per cent in around 36 minutes. One first drive lists 10 to 80 per cent in 46 minutes on a 100kW charger. On 11kW AC, a full charge takes about 10 hours. A vehicle-to-load socket can run power tools, and a heat pump is standard. More in our [charging guide](/guides/charging-an-electric-van/).

### On the road

Reports agree on a reputation for a smooth, car-like ride and a quiet cab, though one test found some bounce from the back when it's empty. Pace is brisk (0-62mph in eight seconds). One test found slow steering, reports agree the driver attention monitor is oversensitive, and another test found the lane-keeping assist tugs at the wheel. Off road, one test found it happier on tarmac: it struggled on a damp grassy slope. There are no off-road modes beyond hill descent control.

### Cab and kit

There's one trim, and it comes loaded: leather, heated and ventilated front seats, heated rear seats, a heated steering wheel, a 360-degree camera and adaptive cruise control. It's also roomy in the back by pickup standards. The let-downs are that the touchscreen is slow and fiddly and phone mirroring needs a cable. Paint other than white costs £750. KGM's Musso EV page gives a seven-year, 90,000-mile vehicle warranty, though the small print on its warranty page names only other models for seven-year cover, so check the terms. The battery is covered for 10 years or 1,000,000 km.

### Alternatives

The [Toyota Hilux electric](/vans/toyota-hilux/) has a better off-road reputation but a much shorter range, and the [Maxus eTerron 9](/vans/maxus-eterron-9/) tows 3.5 tonnes at a much higher price. The [diesel Musso](/vans/kgm-musso/) is the obvious comparison on running costs; try our [electric vs diesel calculator](/tools/electric-vs-diesel/).

### Price

KGM's UK site lists the Musso EV from £39,995 including VAT and the £5,000 Plug-in Van Grant, which makes £44,995 including VAT before the grant. KGM doesn't publish a price excluding VAT, and one of its own pages mentions a £2,500 grant instead, so we haven't scored price. The government lists it in the large-van group for the Plug-in Van Grant, where the grant is worth up to £5,000. The March 2026 launch price was reported as £42,495 including VAT and the grant.
