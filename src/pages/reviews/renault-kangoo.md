The Kangoo E-Tech is the electric version of Renault's small van, and it's the van the [Nissan Townstar EV](/vans/nissan-townstar/) and [Mercedes-Benz eCitan](/vans/mercedes-benz-citan/) are based on. Renault builds it in France, at Maubeuge, and all three share the body, the 45kWh battery and the motor (the Townstar EV shares its underpinnings too, as does the Mercedes, though it has its own cabin). So the real question isn't which one drives best. It's which badge gives you the best price, warranty and dealer.

### Who it's for

Trades, couriers and service fleets doing local and regional work. It's at its best around town: with an official range of 186 miles, long motorway days aren't its strength.

### Load area and payload

There are two lengths: the standard ML has 3.3 cubic metres and the longer LL has 4.2 cubic metres, with a sliding door on each side. Oddly, the longer van carries more, because it gets a heavier-duty rear axle that the short electric van can't have.

Published payload figures don't agree. The ML has been quoted at 608kg and the LL at 764kg, while a July 2026 buying guide put the maximum at 702kg. Ask for the plated figure on the exact van you're quoted for. It can tow up to 1,500kg.

UK vans miss out on the pillarless side opening sold in left-hand-drive markets, and there's no through-load bulkhead for lengths of pipe or timber.

### Range and charging in the real world

A long-term test van managed around 130 miles in winter, dipping to about 100 miles in freezing weather, and around 140 miles as spring arrived. A heat pump is standard, which helps in the cold.

Rapid charging tops out at 80kW, which takes the battery from 20 to 80 per cent in about 40 minutes. That's fine for a lunch-break top-up but slower than several rivals. Our [charging guide](/guides/charging-an-electric-van/) covers home and depot options.

### On the road

The Kangoo E-Tech has a reputation for being smooth, quiet and easy to drive, with a comfortable ride. The brake energy recovery is gentle: even the strongest setting won't bring it to a stop, so there's no one-pedal driving.

### Cab and kit

The climate dials and steering wheel come from the Clio, and the cabin is hard-wearing grey and black plastic rather than plush. A June 2024 update made a reversing camera, an electric parking brake and a digital driver's display standard. Trim names have moved around (Start, Advance and Extra have all been reported), so check what's on the current list. One long-term test van kept dropping its Apple CarPlay connection.

### What's coming

In October 2026 Renault announced an updated Kangoo E-Tech with a bigger 52kWh battery, 100kW DC charging, an official range of up to 354km (about 220 miles, homologation pending) and vehicle-to-load (V2L) power. Renault says prices will follow in the coming weeks. We couldn't confirm when it reaches the UK, or whether the Nissan and Mercedes versions get the same upgrade, so ask your dealer before you order.

### Warranty

This is where the Renault badge loses out. The van warranty is three years or 100,000 miles, extendable at extra cost, while Nissan gives the Townstar EV five years as standard. The battery is covered for eight years, and Renault has said it will replace one that falls below 70% capacity. Servicing is every two years or 24,000 miles.

### Which version to buy

The long LL. It has more space and more payload, and the government's grant list puts the long-wheelbase Kangoo E-Tech on its large-van list, with a discount of up to £5,000, while the medium-wheelbase version is on the small-van list at up to £2,500.
