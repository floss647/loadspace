The Townstar EV is Nissan's version of Renault's Kangoo E-Tech. It shares its underpinnings with the Renault Kangoo and Mercedes Citan, including a 45kWh battery and front-mounted motor, and the Nissan's cabin and touchscreen are nearly identical to the Renault's. It replaces the e-NV200, Nissan's earlier small electric van. So what you're really choosing here is the badge, and with it the warranty, the price and the dealer.

### Who it's for

Urban trades and last-mile delivery work, where the modest range doesn't matter and a quiet, easy van does. It also suits anyone who tows: it can tow 1,500kg, which is a lot for a small electric van.

### Warranty: the reason to pick this badge

Nissan covers the van for five years or 100,000 miles, where Renault gives the [Kangoo E-Tech](/reviews/renault-kangoo/) three years as standard. The battery is covered for eight years with the same 100,000-mile limit. Servicing is every two years or 25,000 miles.

### Load area and payload

There are two lengths: the L1 with 3.3 cubic metres and the L2 with 4.3 cubic metres. The L2's twin sliding doors are wider, at 831mm, and you can load a Euro pallet through them.

Nissan's UK website now gives payloads, including the driver, of 502kg for the L1 electric van and 713kg for the L2. That's well down on the 600kg to 800kg Nissan quoted at launch in 2023, depending on the spec. Either way the L2 carries more. Ask the dealer for the plated payload of the exact van before you sign.

### Range and charging in the real world

Nissan's UK website now quotes up to 178 miles WLTP combined, while its July 2026 price list shows the range as still to be confirmed. Earlier Nissan releases said 183 miles, and a September 2025 Nissan release gave up to 301km (about 187 miles). Either way, the official range is under 200 miles. One road test averaged 3.1 miles per kWh over about 150 miles of mixed UK roads, equivalent to around 139 miles from a full battery.

Rapid charging stops at 80kW, enough for 15 to 80 per cent in 37 minutes, and a 22kW AC wallbox does the same in about 1.5 hours. More in our [charging guide](/guides/charging-an-electric-van/).

### On the road

The Townstar EV has a reputation for being quiet and easy to drive. Reports differ on the ride: one calls it surprisingly supple, another on the firm side of comfortable. There's no one-pedal mode, and wind noise creeps in at higher speeds.

### Cab and kit

Nissan's base Visia and top Tekna+ trims had both been dropped by May 2025, leaving Acenta and Tekna. Acenta has LED headlights, rear parking sensors and cruise control. Tekna adds front, rear and side sensors, keyless entry and go, a phone holder and built-in navigation. Apple CarPlay and Android Auto are standard. The weak spot is the camera kit: the cameras have been criticised for poor picture quality and distorted distances, so don't lean on them.

Acenta trim brings the 22kW on-board charger and 80kW rapid charging, so there's no charging penalty for skipping Tekna.

### Which version to buy

The L2 in Acenta trim. It carries more, its side doors take a pallet, and the government's grant list puts the Townstar L2 on its large-van list, at up to £5,000, while the standard Townstar is on the small-van list at up to £2,500. See our [grants and tax guide](/guides/grants-and-tax/).

### Alternatives

Price up the [Renault Kangoo E-Tech](/reviews/renault-kangoo/) and [Mercedes-Benz eCitan](/reviews/mercedes-benz-citan/) twins, then the Stellantis small vans such as the [Citroën ë-Berlingo](/vans/citroen-berlingo/) and [Toyota Proace City Electric](/vans/toyota-proace-city/). For more range and space, try the [Kia PV5 Cargo](/reviews/kia-pv5-cargo/).

### Price

Nissan's price list, effective from 1 July 2026, starts the Townstar EV at £32,105 before VAT and the grant, for the L1 van in Acenta trim. The L2 Acenta is £33,605, and Tekna adds £1,850. Nissan shows the grant separately: £2,500 on the L1 and £5,000 on the L2. Get written quotes from all three brands, and check how close your nearest Nissan van dealer is: you'll be going back there for servicing and warranty work.
