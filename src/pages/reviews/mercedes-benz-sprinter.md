### Who it's for

The eSprinter is Mercedes' large electric panel van, for fleets and parcel operators who need a lot of space and a long range and can stomach a big bill. The current eSprinter arrived in 2024 and is a very different van from the one sold from 2020, which managed just 96 miles. Few UK road tests of the current van have been published, so this review leans mainly on Mercedes' own UK price list, effective from July 2026. A next-generation Sprinter is expected to arrive in 2027, with electric versions on a new electric platform.

### Load area and payload

Mercedes lists 10.5 cubic metres of load space in the L2 van and 14 cubic metres in the L3, which has a maximum load length of 4,410mm and 1,350mm between the wheelarches.

Payload depends on the weight rating. The 314 and 320 vans are rated at 3,500kg and carry only 495kg to 651kg. The 414 and 420 vans are rated higher: the 81kWh L2 van at 4,150kg carries up to 1,278kg, the 81kWh L3 van 1,229kg and the 113kWh L3 van up to 1,008kg, both at 4,250kg. Mercedes works these out from a kerb weight that includes a driver, and options reduce them, so get the figure for your exact van in writing.

On licences, GOV.UK's rules say a category B licence (test passed on or after 1 January 1997) covers electric or hydrogen vehicles, zero-emission only, up to 4,250kg maximum authorised mass. More in our [van sizes guide](/guides/van-sizes-explained/).

### Range and charging in the real world

Mercedes' list gives the 113kWh 414 L3 van an official WLTP combined range of 272 miles, with 269 and 268 miles for the 420 versions: at launch, more than any other large van promised. The 81kWh vans are rated at 161 to 185 miles. Mercedes calls these preliminary figures that could differ from the official UK values, so check the figure for your spec.

The only real-world figure we found is from a 2024 road test, where an hour-long hilly route with a light load returned 3.1 miles per kWh, and there's no published figure with a heavy load.

Rapid charging runs at up to 115kW. Mercedes quotes 42 minutes from 10 to 80% for the 113kWh battery and 34 minutes for the 81kWh, but public chargers that fast tend to be expensive. On an 11kW wallbox, a full charge of the 113kWh battery takes 11 hours 15 minutes. 22kW AC charging is an extra-cost option that cuts it to six hours. This is a van to charge overnight at base. See our [charging guide](/guides/charging-an-electric-van/).

### On the road

The one detailed UK road test of the current van reports it smooth, comfortable and easy to drive. It found the new D Auto regeneration mode rarely seemed to pick the right setting. There's a choice of 136hp and 204hp motors, with little difference between the two on the road. One test isn't a reputation, though.

### Cab and kit

Every eSprinter has a 10.25-inch MBUX touchscreen with over-the-air updates, and wireless Apple CarPlay and Android Auto, which need Mercedes' Smartphone Integration pack, standard on Pro. There are two trims, Pro and Select. Pro gets a heated driver's seat, air conditioning, pre-entry climate control, a full-length partition and a wood floor. Select adds a comfort driver's seat, a leather steering wheel and LED headlamps. Safety kit on every model includes Active Brake Assist, Blind Spot Assist, Crosswind Assist, Active Lane Keeping Assist and a reversing camera. The Sprinter's Euro NCAP Silver rating comes from a diesel van.

### Which version to buy

Take the 81kWh battery unless your routes need the extra range: the 81kWh 414 L3 Pro van is listed at £60,715, £12,655 less than the same van with the 113kWh battery, and carries more. Pro trim is enough for most fleets. Mercedes includes a three-year unlimited-mileage van warranty, an eight-year or 100,000-mile battery warranty and four services (two minor and two major), and servicing is every year or 24,000 miles.

### Alternatives

Compare it with the [Ford E-Transit](/vans/ford-transit/), the [Renault Master E-Tech](/vans/renault-master/) (the Master was named Parkers Large Van of the Year 2026), the [Vauxhall Movano Electric](/vans/vauxhall-movano/) and its Stellantis twins, the [Iveco eDaily](/vans/iveco-daily/) and the [Maxus eDeliver 9](/vans/maxus-deliver-9/).

### Price

Mercedes' UK price list, effective from July 2026, starts the eSprinter panel van at £58,850 excluding VAT and before the grant, for the 81kWh 314 L3 Pro. The cheapest 113kWh van, the 414 L3 Pro, is £73,370. Delivery charges are extra. The eSprinter is on the GOV.UK list of large vans eligible for the Plug-in Van Grant, where the maximum discount is £5,000 (see our [grants and tax guide](/guides/grants-and-tax/)).
