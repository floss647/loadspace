The eCitan is the Mercedes-Benz version of the Renault Kangoo E-Tech. It shares its bodyshell and running gear with the Renault, including a 45kWh battery and a single motor, and the [Nissan Townstar EV](/vans/nissan-townstar/) is the third twin. Mercedes gives it its own interior and a different aftersales package, and some of its own suspension parts. Few UK road tests of the eCitan have been published, so this review leans mostly on published figures.

### Who it's for

Fleets and businesses that want a Mercedes on the drive, or already deal with a Mercedes van dealer for bigger vans. It suits local work: its official range is 176 miles, where Renault quotes 186 miles for the Kangoo.

### Load area and payload

The standard-length eCitan has 2.9 cubic metres, less than the 3.3 cubic metres of the standard Kangoo. Payload is hard to pin down. At launch it was quoted at 544kg, and a longer version was promised with 722kg. A July 2026 guide gave a figure just over 700kg for all three twins. We couldn't confirm which lengths are on sale now or their payloads, so ask the dealer for the plated figure. It can tow up to 1,450kg, a little less than the Kangoo's 1,500kg.

### Range and charging

Rapid charging peaks at 80kW, so 0 to 80 per cent takes about 35 minutes. A full charge on an 11kW wallbox takes around four and a half hours, and optional 22kW AC charging cuts that to about two and a half. See our [charging guide](/guides/charging-an-electric-van/).

### On the road

It has a reputation for a quiet, easy drive, and a long-term test that put the two side by side found they feel identical on the road. There are only three drive settings (D-, D and D+), fewer than in bigger Mercedes electric vans.

### Cab and kit

This is where the money goes. Every Citan gets six airbags, Active Brake Assist, Attention Assist, rear parking sensors and a reversing camera, and there's an Isofix point with automatic airbag deactivation. One gap: the alarm isn't Thatcham-approved. Reports differ on the MBUX touchscreen, from dated to slick. Trim names have changed since launch, so check the current list.

### Warranty and servicing

The van warranty runs three years with unlimited mileage, and the battery for eight years and 100,000 miles. That's better than Renault for high-mileage drivers, but Nissan gives the Townstar EV five years. At launch Mercedes included the first four services free; ask whether that still applies.

### Price

We couldn't confirm a current UK list price. In December 2023 it was quoted from £33,995 ex VAT before the grant, and from over £33,000 ex VAT reported in May 2025. A July 2026 guide said it has the highest starting price of the three. The eCitan is on the government's small-van grant list, at up to £2,500 (see our [grants and tax guide](/guides/grants-and-tax/)).
