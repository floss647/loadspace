The E-Boxer is Peugeot's version of the big Stellantis electric van. Like the [Vauxhall Movano Electric](/vans/vauxhall-movano/), [Citroën ë-Relay](/vans/citroen-relay/), [Fiat E-Ducato](/vans/fiat-ducato/) and [Toyota Proace Max Electric](/vans/toyota-proace-max/), it is the same van with a different face, and it has the same motor and battery. Before an update at the end of 2024, Peugeot's electric Boxer was converted by an outside company, so don't judge the current van by older reviews. We found no detailed UK road test of the current electric van on its own: the one detailed review we found covers the whole Boxer range, diesel included.

### Who it's for

Fleets and trades who want a big electric van and a simple choice. There's one trim, Professional, and it comes in four panel van sizes: L3H2, L3H3, L4H2 and L4H3. The L3H2 is also sold as a 3,500kg van; every other version is 4,250kg. If you want a factory conversion, Fiat's Ducato offers more choice.

### Load area and payload

Payload is the figure to check. Peugeot's guide lists 730kg for the 3,500kg L3H2 and 1,385kg to 1,480kg for the 4,250kg versions. Peugeot's payloads don't include the driver, so the driver's weight comes out of them, and they don't allow for options you add. Ask for the plated weight of the exact van you're ordering. If you're looking at a heavier version, note that under the current GOV.UK rules a standard category B car licence covers electric vehicles up to 4,250kg, including one gained on or after 1 January 1997 (the limit for petrol and diesel vans is 3,500kg). The 4,250kg vans are N2 vehicles, so they are limited to 56mph and need a tachograph. It can tow up to 2,400kg, but Peugeot doesn't offer a tow bar on the electric van, so you'll need an aftermarket fitting. The gross train weight is 5,500kg, so a fully loaded 4,250kg van can pull only 1,250kg.

### Range and charging

Peugeot quotes a range of 252 to 263 miles for the 4,250kg vans and 235 miles for the 3,500kg van. The E-Boxer is homologated as a heavy vehicle, so it isn't WLTP tested: Peugeot calculates these figures using the WLTP method. Rapid charging at up to 150kW takes it from 5 to 80% in 55 minutes. It has a 22kW on-board charger, and Peugeot quotes a full charge in 6 hours 5 minutes on a 22kW wallbox, while a 7.4kW wallbox needs 16 hours 40 minutes. When it is heavily loaded, expect it to be best kept to shorter trips and urban work. More in our [charging guide](/guides/charging-an-electric-van/).

### On the road

This is where the E-Boxer earns its place, according to the one detailed review we found of the Boxer range. It rates the E-Boxer the best Boxer to drive by far. The motor is smooth, quiet and strong, and the battery weight makes it more comfortable when empty than the diesel. The design is old, though, and wind noise builds at motorway speeds. A reversing camera isn't standard on the electric van: it's a £200 option, and worth having on a van this size.

### Cab and kit

Standard kit includes automatic air conditioning, rear parking sensors, electrically adjustable heated door mirrors, a 7-inch digital instrument display and a 7-inch touchscreen with Apple CarPlay and Android Auto. Cruise control isn't standard on the electric van: the Easy Driving Pack adds adaptive cruise control for £500, or the Level 2 Autonomous Driving pack adds it with lane centring for £700. A 10-inch screen with navigation comes in the £900 Techno Pack. All prices here exclude VAT.

### Warranty and price

You get three years or 100,000 miles on the van, and Peugeot covers the battery for 8 years or 100,000 miles, to 70% of its capacity. Among the twins, the Toyota Proace Max Electric has longer cover.

Peugeot's price list from 1 October 2026 starts at £49,735 excluding VAT for the 3,500kg L3H2 and runs to £52,165 for the L4H3, both before the grant. The E-Boxer is on the government's list of large vans eligible for the van grant, worth up to £5,000 (see our [grants and tax guide](/guides/grants-and-tax/)). Our [electric vs diesel calculator](/tools/electric-vs-diesel/) shows whether it pays for your mileage.

### Alternatives

Price up the four twins first. Outside the family, try the [Renault Master E-Tech](/vans/renault-master/), [Ford E-Transit](/vans/ford-transit/) and [Mercedes-Benz eSprinter](/vans/mercedes-benz-sprinter/).
