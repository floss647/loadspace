### Who it's for

The Proace City Electric is for small businesses that want a small electric van for local work and plan to keep it for years. It's built by Stellantis and is technically the same van as the [Citroën ë-Berlingo](/vans/citroen-berlingo/), [Peugeot E-Partner](/vans/peugeot-partner/), [Vauxhall Combo Electric](/vans/vauxhall-combo/) and [Fiat E-Doblo](/vans/fiat-doblo/): Toyota doesn't belong to Stellantis but has the right to sell the van under its own badge. That pedigree counts for something: the Proace City was named the 2024 Parkers Small Van of the Year.

### What we could and couldn't confirm

Few UK road tests of the current, facelifted electric Proace City have been published, so this review is shorter than usual and leans on what is shared with its siblings. When the facelifted Proace City van range was announced in March 2024, it was diesel only at first. The Proace City Electric is now on the government's small-van grant list, which takes up to £2,500 off at the dealer.

A July 2026 buying guide covering all five badges together gives a maximum payload of 781kg, rapid charging at up to 100kW, and a range of 220 miles in one place and 213 miles in another. Those are figures for the family, not Toyota's own, and the range doesn't agree with itself, so we haven't scored them. Ask Toyota for the exact payload, range and charging spec of the van you're quoted. For what the shared van is like to load and drive, see our [Vauxhall Combo Electric review](/reviews/vauxhall-combo/) and [Citroën ë-Berlingo review](/reviews/citroen-berlingo/).

### The warranty, which is the point

The same guide says the Toyota has the edge over its siblings on maintenance, and this is why. When Toyota Relax launched in 2021, it was reported to work like this: once the initial three-year basic warranty runs out, each Toyota service renews full manufacturer cover until the next one is due, as long as the van is under 10 years old and has covered fewer than 100,000 miles. Skip a Toyota service and that extra cover stops, so it suits buyers happy to use the dealer network.

Its siblings get three years on the van and eight years or 100,000 miles on the battery. We couldn't confirm Toyota's battery warranty for this van, or whether Relax covers the battery, so ask before you sign.

### Price

We couldn't find a current list price, so get a written quote and compare it with the other four badges, putting the cost of Toyota servicing against the longer cover.
