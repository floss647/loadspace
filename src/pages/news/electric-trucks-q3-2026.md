The UK truck market finally grew again in the third quarter of 2026. **9,941 new trucks** were registered, up **7.2%**, the first quarterly rise since 2024, according to SMMT figures reported by trade press.

Zero-emission trucks went the other way. Registrations **fell by about half** and their share of the market dropped to **1.1%**, down from 2.4% a year earlier. Across the first nine months of 2026, zero-emission truck registrations are down **30.9%**, at about **1%** of the market.

## Why

- **Large government-funded trials have ended**, which had been propping up orders in 2025.
- **Prices are still far higher** than diesel trucks.
- **Depot power is a bottleneck.** Getting a big enough grid connection can take a long time.

## What it means for operators

- **The new Zero Emission Truck Grant** can cover **up to 40%** of a new electric truck's price, with caps by weight that reach about **£81,000** for the heaviest trucks. Check current amounts with your dealer.
