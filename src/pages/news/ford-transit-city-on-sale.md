Ford has added a cheaper electric van to its range. The **Transit City** is **electric only** and sits between the small E-Transit Courier and the E-Transit Custom.

## The key numbers

- **Price:** about **£29,000** for the L1 van and about **£31,500** for the taller L2H2 version, excluding VAT, according to UK reports. The E-Transit Custom starts at about £43,600.
- **Range:** around **160 miles**.
- **Payload:** **1,085kg** (L1H1) and up to **1,275kg** (L2H2).
- **Bodies:** two van sizes plus a chassis cab for conversions.
- **On sale:** now. Ford expected all versions in dealers from October 2026, with Ford's own conversions following in early 2027.

It's built in China, which is how Ford gets the price down, and it's aimed at urban delivery and trades that don't need a big range.

## What it means for you

At about £29,000, the Transit City **undercuts a diesel Transit Custom**. For local work with home or depot charging, it could be one of the cheapest ways into a medium-sized electric van.
