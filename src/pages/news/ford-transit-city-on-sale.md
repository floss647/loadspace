Ford has added a cheaper electric van to its range. The **Transit City** is **electric only**, and it sits between the small E-Transit Courier and the E-Transit Custom. At **£29,421 before VAT**, it costs about £14,000 less than an E-Transit Custom and undercuts a basic diesel Transit Custom.

Ford keeps the price down in three ways:

- **Where it's built.** The van is made in China by Jiangling Motors (JMC), a company Ford part-owns and has worked with for more than 30 years. It's based on a JMC van, though Ford says the battery and drive system are its own.
- **One spec.** Every van comes the same way, with no options list.
- **A smaller battery** than most rivals.

## The key numbers

| | L1H1 (standard) | L2H2 (long, high roof) |
|---|---|---|
| Price, excluding VAT | £29,421 | £31,921 |
| Official range (WLTP) | 158 miles | 139 miles |
| Load volume | 6.4m³ | 8.5m³ |
| Payload | 1,085kg | 1,275kg |

Prices are as reported when the van went on sale in September. Early launch coverage quoted £29,000 and £31,500, so check the latest figure with a dealer.

- **Battery:** 56kWh, using LFP (lithium iron phosphate) chemistry. LFP cells are cheaper and handle regular charging to 100% better than the batteries in many rivals.
- **Motor:** 150PS (110kW), front-wheel drive. Top speed is limited to 75mph.
- **Charging:**
  - **Rapid charging:** up to 87kW, so **10–80% takes about 33 minutes**.
  - **Wallbox:** 11kW AC, which fills it in about five hours. On a typical 7kW home charger, allow about eight hours, which is fine overnight.
- **Kit:**
  - 12-inch touchscreen with wireless Apple CarPlay and Android Auto
  - air conditioning
  - heated driver's seat
  - keyless start
  - reversing camera and parking sensors
  - adaptive cruise control
- **Battery warranty:** 8 years or 100,000 miles. Servicing is every two years or 25,000 miles.
- **Towing:** reports say it isn't designed to tow. If you pull a trailer, check before ordering.

## Bodies and conversions

Two van lengths are on sale now. Box van, dropside and racked versions are offered through Ford from launch, with refrigerated and tipper bodies due in 2027. A **chassis cab** for other conversions was shown at the IAA Transportation show in September and follows later. It can carry up to 1,600kg.

## Does it get the van grant?

It weighs about 3 tonnes fully loaded, which puts it in the **large van band** of the [Zero Emission Van Grant](/news/zero-emission-van-grant-explained/), worth up to £5,000. Ford's UK pricing reportedly reflects the grant, which would bring the starting price to **about £24,000 before VAT**. Make sure the dealer confirms the grant on your quote, and check that the van is on the government's eligible list.

## How it compares

Approximate starting prices before VAT, after the van grant, as reported by UK dealers and price lists:

- **Ford Transit City:** about £24,400
- **Kia PV5 Cargo:** about £27,400, with more range
- **Vauxhall Vivaro Electric:** about £36,100
- **Citroën ë-Dispatch:** about £37,700
- **Ford E-Transit Custom:** about £38,600

## What it means for you

The Transit City is built for **local work**: urban delivery, couriers and trades who rarely cover more than 100 miles a day and can charge at home or at the depot.

- **The compromise is range.** 158 miles on paper means more like 110–120 in winter with a load on board, and less again for the long-wheelbase van.
- **Do long motorway days?** The [E-Transit Custom](/vans/ford-transit-custom/) or a Stellantis medium van with a bigger battery will suit you better.
- **Nobody has published a full UK road test yet.** We'll add driving impressions to our [Transit City page](/vans/ford-transit-city/) when we've driven one.
