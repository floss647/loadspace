Ford's new **Transit City** is now **on sale in the UK**. It's electric only, close in size to a Transit Custom, and much cheaper. Ford's UK price list starts it at **£29,000 before VAT**, or **£29,436** before VAT with on-the-road costs.

That's **£14,630 less** than the cheapest E-Transit Custom, which Ford lists from £43,630 before VAT. Both prices are before the van grant.

Ford keeps the price down in three ways:

- **Where it's built.** The van is **made in Nanchang, China**, by **Ford's Chinese partner JMC**, Van Reviewer reports. The **batteries come from CATL**, a Chinese supplier.
- **One spec.** There's a single trim level, so there's no long options list.
- **A smaller battery.** It has a **56kWh** battery. Ford says that's plenty for city work, and Auto Express reports Ford's data shows current electric van users drive **less than 70 miles a day** on average.

## The key numbers

| | L1H1 (short, low roof) | L2H2 (long, high roof) |
|---|---|---|
| List price, before VAT | £29,000 | £31,500 |
| On-the-road price, before VAT | £29,436 | £31,936 |
| Official range (WLTP) | 158 miles | 139 miles |
| Load volume | 6.4m³ | 8.5m³ |
| Payload | 1,085kg | 1,275kg |
| Gross weight | 3,000kg | 3,270kg |

Figures are from Ford UK's website and its price list dated 29 April 2026. Prices can change, so check with a dealer.

- **Battery:** 56kWh of usable capacity, using LFP (lithium iron phosphate) cells.
- **Range in town:** Ford quotes up to 236.7 miles for the short van on the WLTP city test. Real-world range will be lower in cold weather or on faster roads.
- **Motor:** 110kW (150PS) and 260Nm, driving the front wheels. Auto Express reports 0-62mph in 13.7 seconds and a top speed of 75mph.
- **Charging:**
  - **Rapid charging:** up to 87kW. Ford says 10–80% takes 33 minutes.
  - **Depot charging:** 11kW three-phase takes 5.5 hours for 10–100%, according to Ford. On a 7.4kW single-phase charger, allow 8 hours.
- **Kit:** a 12.3-inch touchscreen with Apple CarPlay and Android Auto (connected by USB), air conditioning, a heated driver's seat, keyless start, front and rear parking sensors, a reversing camera and adaptive cruise control.
- **Warranty:** three years or 100,000 miles on the van, according to Ford's price list. Trade press reports an eight-year warranty on the high-voltage parts, but reports differ on the mileage limit.
- **Servicing:** every two years, with no mileage limit, according to Ford's price list.
- **Towing:** Van Reviewer reports it can't tow. If you pull a trailer, this isn't your van.

## Bodies and conversions

There are two panel vans. A chassis cab is on the price list too, at £31,500 before VAT, and Ford says it can carry up to 1,600kg. Auto Express reports that tipper and Luton-style conversions will come from existing converters, and Van Reviewer reports a refrigerated body is due from early 2027.

## Does it get the van grant?

Yes. The **Ford Transit City Van** is on the government's list of large vans for the [Zero Emission Van Grant](/news/zero-emission-van-grant-explained/), worth up to £5,000. Large vans must weigh between 2,500kg and 4,250kg fully loaded, and both Transit City vans fall in that band. Ford's UK website also advertises the £5,000 grant saving.

Taking £5,000 off the £29,000 list price gives £24,000. But Ford's website currently shows the short van at **£22,200** for VAT-registered buyers. That's £1,800 below our sum, and we couldn't see on the page how Ford works it out. Ask your dealer for a written quote that shows the grant and VAT clearly.

## What it means for you

The Transit City is built for **local work**: urban delivery, couriers and trades who can charge at home or at the depot.

- **The compromise is range.** 158 miles is the official figure for the short van, and the long van is rated lower.
- **Need to tow or do long motorway days?** Van Reviewer reports the [E-Transit Custom](/vans/ford-transit-custom/) can tow up to 2.5 tonnes, and it has a bigger battery.
- **We haven't driven one yet.** We'll add our impressions to our [Transit City page](/vans/ford-transit-city/) when we have.
