The government's consultation on the **zero-emission vehicle (ZEV) mandate**, the rule requiring a rising share of each maker's new vans to be electric, closed on **23 October 2026** after ten weeks.

Under current rules, **24%** of new vans must be zero-emission this year, rising to **46% in 2028** and **70% by 2030**. Actual sales are running at about 11%.

## What's on the table

- **Lower the 2030 van target** to **60%, 50% or 40%**, with steeper increases after 2030, or
- **Keep 70%** but extend flexibilities, such as buying credits, until 2034.

The government's own consultation document admits forecasts point to 2030 sales below the current targets. The dealer body NFDA has asked for **40% for vans** in 2030. The end dates for new diesel vans are **not** part of the review.

## What it means for you

- **Nothing changes yet.** The current targets apply until the government publishes its decision.
- **A lower target could mean fewer discounts.** Much of today's discounting on electric vans comes from makers trying to hit the mandate. If the target is cut, that pressure eases.
- **If you're close to switching,** today's deals may be as good as they get for a while. Compare the options with our [lease, buy new or buy used calculator](/tools/lease-or-buy/).
