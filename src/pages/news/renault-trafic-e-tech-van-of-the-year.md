The all-new **Renault Trafic E-Tech** has been named **International Van of the Year 2027**, chosen unanimously by a panel of 26 trade journalists from 26 European countries.

Unlike the old Trafic, which was diesel only, the new one is built as an electric van from the ground up.

## What we know so far

- **Batteries:** a long-range **81kWh** version first, with a cheaper **60kWh** version following in **early 2027**.
- **European prices:** from about **€41,500 to €47,000** before VAT in France. UK prices hadn't been published when we checked.
- **UK version:** coming in a single **Advance** trim, according to What Van?.

## What it means for you

The Trafic E-Tech is a serious new option in the busiest part of the market, medium vans. It will compete with the [Ford E-Transit Custom](/vans/ford-transit-custom/), the [Vauxhall Vivaro Electric](/vans/vauxhall-vivaro/) and its twins, and the new Ford Transit City. Its twin, the Nissan Primastar, is also expected to go electric.

If you're due to change a medium van in 2027, it's worth waiting for UK prices and lease deals before committing.
