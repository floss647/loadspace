The all-new **Renault Trafic E-Tech** has been named **International Van of the Year 2027**. Every one of the 26 jurors, trade van journalists from across Europe, gave it their top marks. The award was presented at the IAA Transportation show in Hanover in September. It's the 35th year of the award and, by Renault's count, its sixth win.

The Stellantis ProOne Compact came second, with the Farizon V7E and Volkswagen Caddy Cargo joint third.

## A new kind of Trafic

This isn't the familiar Trafic with a battery added. The E-Tech sits on a **new platform designed only for electric vans**, with the battery under the floor and the motor at the back. It's built in Sandouville, France, alongside an updated diesel Trafic, which stays on sale as a separate model.

Renault calls it its first **"software-defined" van**, meaning much of how it works can be updated over the air, like a phone. For fleets, that should mean fixes and new features without a trip to the dealer.

## The key numbers

Renault's figures are provisional until the van is officially tested.

| | 60kWh battery | 81kWh battery |
|---|---|---|
| Official range (WLTP) | up to 350km (about 215 miles) | over 470km (about 290 miles) |
| Arrives | early 2027 | first |

- **Motor:** 150kW (about 200hp), driving the rear wheels.
- **Charging:** an **800-volt** system that takes up to **190kW** on a rapid charger, so the 81kWh van goes from **15% to 80% in about 20 minutes**. That's quicker than almost any van on sale today.
- **Load space:** 5.1m³ in the standard L1 and 5.8m³ in the longer L2.
- **Payload:** about 1.25 tonnes.
- **Towing:** 2 tonnes.
- **Turning circle:** reported at 10.3 metres, similar to a small hatchback, which helps in town.
- **Power out:** the higher trim adds 230-volt sockets in the cab and load area, so you can run tools from the van's battery.
- **Body types:** panel vans first, with chassis cab and crew cab versions in 2027.

## Prices and the UK

In France, prices start from about **€41,500 before VAT** for the 60kWh van and about **€47,000** for the 81kWh. The better-equipped trim costs about €2,000 more.

The production van made its UK debut at the CV Show in Birmingham in April. Right-hand-drive vans are expected in **early 2027**, but **UK prices and trims haven't been announced yet**. The outgoing Trafic E-Tech cost £39,500 before VAT and before the grant when it launched in 2024. That's a rough guide, not a prediction.

As a medium van weighing more than 2.5 tonnes loaded, it should qualify for the larger **£5,000** [Zero Emission Van Grant](/news/zero-emission-van-grant-explained/), if Renault lists it as eligible.

The Nissan Primastar, the Trafic's long-time twin, hasn't been confirmed in an electric version. Renault Trucks is selling its own badged version.
