The government's discount on new electric vans has a new name. The **Zero Emission Van Grant** and **Zero Emission Truck Grant** are an updated version of the old Plug-in Van Grant and Plug-in Truck Grant, paid by the Office for Zero Emission Vehicles.

According to the government's subsidy assessment, published in March 2026, the scheme runs from **1 April 2026 to 31 March 2030**, or until its **£877 million** budget runs out. It was announced on 25 March 2026 as part of a **£1 billion** package for electric vans, trucks and depot chargers.

## Vans

| Van | Gross vehicle weight | Grant |
|---|---|---|
| Small | Under 2,500kg | Up to £2,500 |
| Large | 2,500kg to 4,250kg | Up to £5,000 |

To qualify, a van must produce zero CO2 at the tailpipe, travel at least 60 miles (96km) without any emissions, and be on the government's approved list.

Small vans on the list include the Citroën e-Berlingo and Vauxhall Combo-e. Large ones include the Renault Trafic E-Tech, Kia PV5 Cargo and some versions of the Ford E-Transit Custom. New versions of an approved van aren't automatically covered, so check the exact model and trim. Browse electric models on our [van pages](/vans/).

## Trucks

In England, Scotland and Wales, the grant is a cash cap or a share of the upfront cost:

| Truck weight | Most you can get | Old Plug-in Truck Grant cap |
|---|---|---|
| 4.25 to 12 tonnes | £15,000 or 20%, whichever is lower | £20,000 |
| 12 to 18 tonnes | £37,000 or 40%, whichever is lower | £60,000 |
| 18 to 26 tonnes | £52,000 or 40%, whichever is lower | £80,000 |
| Over 26 tonnes | £81,000 or 40%, whichever is lower | £120,000 |

Old caps are from the government's subsidy assessment. In Northern Ireland, small and medium-sized firms get the same rates, but large firms are capped at 30% for trucks over 12 tonnes. Smaller firms there must supply a signed declaration that they qualify.

## How you get it

- **The dealer does it.** The dealer or manufacturer takes it off the price at the point of sale. There's no form for you to fill in.
- **Buying or leasing** both count.
- **New only.** The grant is for new vans and trucks at first registration. Used vehicles don't qualify. Conversions done after registration don't count.
- **Timing matters.** The dealer must put your order on the government's grant portal before the vehicle is registered, and not on the same day.
- **Your rate is protected.** The grant is locked in at the level advertised when the dealer puts your order on the portal, as long as the vehicle is registered and delivered within 12 months.
- **What "price" means.** The grant comes off the purchase price after any discount. For this, the price includes VAT (where it applies), number plates and road tax, but not optional extras or delivery. Ask for an invoice with the grant shown separately.

## Limits and catches

- **Annual cap.** Each business, organisation or individual can get up to 1,500 van grants and 100 truck grants each financial year (1 April to 31 March).
- **Leased vehicles count against you,** the end customer, not the leasing company.
- **Linked companies** in the same group can be treated as one business.
- **Orders over the limit are cancelled,** and the government can claw the money back if a customer goes over.
- **UK only.** The vehicle must be registered in the UK, with UK VAT paid. The Isle of Man, Jersey and Guernsey aren't included.
- **Grant levels and rules can change** at the government's discretion, and the money is limited.

## Other help

- **Depot chargers.** The same package gave the Depot Charging Scheme £170 million. The government says businesses can get up to 70% of the cost, up to £1 million, for chargers for vans, coaches and electric HGVs. See our [depot charging guide](/guides/depot-charging/).
- **Workplace chargers.** The Workplace Charging Scheme pays up to £500 per socket, for up to 40 sockets across your sites, until 31 March 2027.

## Background

The Plug-in Van Grant launched in 2012. By February 2025, the government said it had helped sell over 80,000 electric and zero emission vans.
