From **1 April 2026**, the old Plug-in Van and Truck Grants were replaced by the **Zero Emission Van Grant** and the **Zero Emission Truck Grant**.

## Vans

- **Up to £2,500** off a small electric van (up to 2.5 tonnes).
- **Up to £5,000** off a large electric van (2.5 to 4.25 tonnes).
- The discount is applied by the dealer on eligible **new** vans. Used vans don't qualify.
- There are limits on how many grants one business can claim each year, and the government can change amounts or eligibility.

## Trucks

- **Up to 40%** of the price of a new zero-emission truck, with caps that depend on weight. Trade press reports caps of up to about **£81,000** for the heaviest trucks.

## How long it lasts

The schemes run until **31 March 2030**, or until the money runs out.

Check the van is on the government's eligible list and ask the dealer to confirm the grant on the quote. For tax, VAT and other incentives, see our [grants and tax guide](/guides/grants-and-tax/).
