September is always the biggest month for new vans because of the new number plate, and 2026 was no different: **49,704 new vans, pickups and 4x4s** were registered, up 4.8% on last year and the busiest month of 2026 so far, according to the motor industry body SMMT.

Electric vans had their best month ever, with **4,832 registrations, up 13.4%**. But because the whole market grew too, they made up just **9.6%** of sales in September and **10.8%** so far this year.

That's well short of the **24%** the government's zero-emission target requires van makers to hit in 2026. SMMT blames higher upfront prices, the cost of upgrading depots and patchy access to reliable charging, and says more than 40 electric van models are now on sale.

## What it means for you

- **Expect deals to keep coming.** Makers who miss the target face penalties or have to buy credits, so they have every reason to discount electric vans and lease deals.
- **The grant still applies.** Up to £2,500 off small vans and £5,000 off large vans under the [Zero Emission Van Grant](/news/zero-emission-van-grant-explained/).
